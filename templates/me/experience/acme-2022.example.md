## Context

Acme Analytics sells a usage-based analytics product to mid-size retailers. Billing is computed nightly from product events, so a late or wrong pipeline run means late or wrong invoices. I joined the 4-person data platform team to stabilise billing and make the warehouse trustworthy for the analysts.

## Team & scale

- Data platform team of 4 engineers; I report to the head of data. Two junior engineers joined in 2023 and I mentor both.
- About 25 analysts and 3 finance staff consume the warehouse daily.
- Product events: roughly 40 million per day into Kafka at peak season.
- 120 dbt models in Snowflake by the end of 2023.

## What I did

- Rebuilt the nightly billing pipeline as Airflow DAGs on Snowflake, replacing a chain of cron jobs and stored procedures.
- Introduced dbt tests and data contracts, so a broken upstream schema fails in CI before it reaches a dashboard.
- Built a Kafka-to-Snowflake ingestion service for product events in Python, packaged with Docker and deployed with Terraform.
- Wrote the team's on-call handbook and run the weekly incident review.
- Mentor two junior engineers through code review and pairing.

## Tools

Python, SQL, Airflow, dbt, Snowflake, Kafka, Docker, Terraform, GitHub Actions

## Outcomes

- Billing pipeline run time went from 6 hours to 40 minutes (Airflow run history, Q3 2022).
- Zero late invoices in 2023, against 7 late billing runs in 2021 (finance ops report).
- dbt test coverage across all 120 models.

## Numbers I'm unsure about

- The new ingestion service probably cut warehouse load cost by around a third. Snowflake credits for ingestion dropped from roughly 900 to roughly 600 a month, but other changes shipped at the same time.
- Analysts used to spend maybe 2 hours a week each re-running broken dashboards. I think about 10 of the 25 were affected.

## Anything else

- Promoted from Data Engineer I to Data Engineer II in January 2024 (title on the resume stays "Data Engineer").
- Gave an internal talk on data contracts that two other teams adopted.
