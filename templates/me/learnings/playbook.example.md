# Resume playbook

Generated 2026-06-01 from 41 submitted applications: 9 callbacks, 17 rejections, 11 ghosted, 4 still pending. Baseline callback rate 24% (n=37, moderate). Fictional example data.

## How to read this

- A callback is an assessment, interview or offer. Ghosted means 21+ days with no reply.
- Patterns with fewer than 3 resolved applications are never shown. **weak** means n < 8; **moderate** means n >= 8.
- These are correlations, not guarantees. Fact rules always win: only confirmed metrics and master bullets.

## Do more of this

- Lead with the **impact** angle on backend and platform roles: 6/14 callbacks (43%, lift 1.8x; n=14, moderate).
- Open current-role bullets with a number plus the mechanism ("cut X by Y using Z", `shape:xyz`): 8/22 callbacks (36%, lift 1.5x; n=22, moderate).
- Keep the Northwind checkout-latency bullet [northwind-2021.b02] in the top three: 5/11 callbacks (45%, lift 1.9x; n=11, moderate).

## Do less of this

- Generic headlines ("Software Engineer" with no stack or domain, framing `generalist`): 1/10 callbacks (10%, lift 0.4x; n=10, moderate).

## Hints (weak evidence)
